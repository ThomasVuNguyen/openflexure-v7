# OpenFlexure v7.0.0-beta5 hardware buy list

Build: **standard low-cost motorized microscope**, using the Raspberry Pi Camera lens. This is the hardware, O-rings, and assembly supplies list; it does not include printed parts, electronics, or optics.

## Order checklist

| Done | Part | Minimum to have | Example listing |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| [ ] | M3 × 10 mm socket-head cap screws, A2/18-8 stainless, 2.5 mm hex drive | 18 | [Bolt Depot #6380](https://boltdepot.com/Product-Details?product=6380) |
| [ ] | M3 × 25 mm fully threaded hex-head bolts, A2/18-8 stainless | 4 | [Bolt Depot #11104](https://boltdepot.com/Product-Details?product=11104) |
| [ ] | M3 × 0.5 standard hex nuts, steel or stainless | 22 | [Bolt Depot #4783, zinc-plated steel](https://boltdepot.com/Product-Details?product=4783) |
| [ ] | M3 standard hex nuts, **brass** | 3 | [Timesavers 10-pack](https://timesavers.com/i-8946813-brass-hex-nut-m3-10-pack.html) |
| [ ] | M3 Form A flat washers, A2/18-8 stainless | 8 | [Bolt Depot #4513](https://boltdepot.com/Product-Details?product=4513) |
| [ ] | No. 2 × 6.5 mm self-tapping **pan-head** screws, approximately 2.2 mm diameter | 20 | [Bolt Depot #9861, Phillips #2 × 1/4 in](https://boltdepot.com/Product-Details?product=9861) |
| [ ] | FKM/Viton O-rings, **30 mm inside diameter × 2 mm cross-section** (34 mm outside diameter), about 75 Shore A | 3 | [O-Rings and More 30 × 2 mm FKM](https://oringsandmore.com/fkm-o-ring-30-x-2mm-minimum-2-pcs/) |

The **18 cap screws** include 17 installed plus 1 used as an assembly tool. The **22 steel M3 nuts** include 21 in the bill of materials (including the printer-fit test) plus 1 used as an assembly tool. If sold in packs, buy at least the listed quantity. A few spare self-tappers are sensible because their heads can strip.

The official self-tappers are Pozidriv; the linked US screws are Phillips and nominally 1/4 in (6.35 mm) long. [OpenFlexure says other drive types such as Phillips work](../docs/parts/mechanical.yml), provided you use the matching screwdriver. Do not substitute flat-head wood screws.

## Assembly supplies

| Done | Item | Need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ ] | 2.5 mm **ball-end** hex key | 1; check your existing tools first |
| [ ] | PZ1 screwdriver for official Pozidriv self-tappers, or matching Phillips driver for Phillips self-tappers | 1 |
